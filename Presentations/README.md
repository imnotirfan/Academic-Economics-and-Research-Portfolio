## Research Presentations

### 1. Effects of Ride-Sharing Employment on Educational Outcomes

**Focus:** Labour Economics · Gig Economy · Human Capital · Opportunity Cost

A primary survey examining the relationship between ride-sharing employment and educational outcomes among private university students in Dhaka.

The study compares students working more than 5 hours per day with those working 5 hours or less, examining **academic performance, absenteeism, health outcomes, and dropout considerations**.

**Methods & Tools**

* Primary survey research
* Descriptive statistics
* Comparative analysis
* Opportunity-cost analysis
* Economic cost-benefit modelling

**Sample:** 40 student drivers across 15 private universities in Dhaka

### 2. Empowering Divorced and Single Mothers in Bangladesh

**Focus:** Development Economics · Gender Economics · Social Policy · Poverty & Inequality

A **Problem Tree Analysis** examining the structural, economic, social, and institutional barriers affecting divorced and single mothers in Bangladesh.

The framework connects **root causes → economic and social exclusion → potential consequences → policy interventions**, with particular attention to employment, financial inclusion, legal access, social norms, and childcare.

**Methods & Tools**

* Problem Tree Analysis
* Root-cause mapping
* Cause-and-effect analysis
* Socio-economic diagnostics
* Policy intervention design
