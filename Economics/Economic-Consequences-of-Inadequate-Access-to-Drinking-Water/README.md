# The Economic and Social Consequences of Inadequate Access to Clean Drinking Water in Teota

## Overview

This field-based report examines how inadequate access to clean drinking water affects the **economic and social conditions** of communities in Teota, Manikganj.

Based on **on-site field observations**, the report explores the links between water insecurity, health, household costs, labour allocation, livestock, education, and broader economic vulnerability.

## Key Findings

* Contaminated and unreliable water sources create **health and household costs**.
* Time spent collecting water reduces **productive labour and income opportunities**.
* Poor water quality can affect **livestock-dependent households**.
* Water insecurity can reinforce **educational disruption and intergenerational poverty**.
* River erosion and displacement make access to basic infrastructure more difficult.
* Long-term resilience requires investment in **safe water, sanitation, healthcare, and basic public services**.
