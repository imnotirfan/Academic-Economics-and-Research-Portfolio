## Selected Coursework

### 1. Economic Consequences of Inadequate Access to Drinking Water

**Focus:** Development Economics · Public Economics · Externalities · Human Capital

An analysis of the economic consequences associated with inadequate access to safe drinking water, examining how water insecurity can affect **health, productivity, household costs, education, and economic development**.

**Key Areas**

* Water insecurity and economic welfare
* Health and labour productivity
* Household economic costs
* Human capital
* Development policy
