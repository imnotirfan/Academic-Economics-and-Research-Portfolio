## Research & Selected Projects

### Testing the Solow Growth Model: Bangladesh & Malaysia

A comparative analysis of long-run economic growth in Bangladesh and Malaysia using the Solow Growth Model, examining differences in capital accumulation, productivity, and economic performance.

**Methods & Skills:** Economic modelling · Comparative analysis · Economic data analysis · Academic research

---

### Social Media Advertisement & Consumer Perception – East West University Students

A survey-based study examining university students' perceptions of social media advertising in East West University Students.

The project involved questionnaire design, primary data collection, analysis of survey responses, and interpretation of consumer perceptions.

**Methods & Skills:** Survey design · Primary data collection · Statistical analysis · Consumer research

---

### Debt Crisis Analysis: Sri Lanka, Lebanon & Venezuela

A comparative analysis of major sovereign debt crises, examining their economic causes, policy responses, and consequences for macroeconomic stability and development.

**Methods & Skills:** Comparative research · Macroeconomic analysis · Policy analysis · Literature review
