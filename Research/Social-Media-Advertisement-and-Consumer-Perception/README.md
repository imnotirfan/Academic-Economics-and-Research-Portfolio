# Social Media Advertisement and Consumer Perception in Bangladesh

## Overview

This research examines **consumer perceptions of social media advertising among East West University students in Bangladesh**. It investigates social media usage, advertisement preferences, factors influencing purchase decisions, and attitudes toward digital advertising.

The study focuses on how factors such as **advertisement relevance, visual appeal, promotional offers, brand credibility, and influencer marketing** influence consumer responses.

## Research Objectives

* Identify commonly used social media platforms among university students.
* Examine advertising features that influence purchase intention.
* Analyze students' perceptions and attitudes toward social media advertising.
* Investigate the influence of pop-up ads, influencers, and video advertisements.
* Develop practical insights for digital marketers targeting young consumers.

## Key Findings

* **96.0%** of respondents accessed social media daily.
* **69.2%** had made a purchase influenced by a social media advertisement.
* **42.3%** reported being somewhat influenced by celebrity or influencer endorsements.
* **46.2%** expressed mixed feelings toward digital advertising.
* Brand reputation and perceived relevance were important factors in consumer decision-making.
* Visual and video-based advertising received stronger preference than simpler advertising formats.

## Research Approach

The study combines **primary and secondary research**:

* **Primary Data:** Structured questionnaire administered through Google Forms to East West University students.
* **Secondary Research:** Review of existing literature on social media marketing and consumer behavior.
* **Analysis:** Descriptive quantitative analysis using frequencies and percentages.
