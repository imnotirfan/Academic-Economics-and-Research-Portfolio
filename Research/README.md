# Research Portfolio

This section contains selected academic research projects completed during my undergraduate studies in **Economics at East West University**.

The projects cover a range of topics in **economic growth, macroeconomics, development economics, international economics, consumer behaviour, labour markets, and economic policy**. They demonstrate my experience with economic research, literature review, comparative analysis, primary data collection, survey design, quantitative analysis, and academic writing.

---

## Selected Research Projects

### [Testing the Solow Growth Model: Bangladesh & Malaysia](./Testing-Solow-Growth/)

**Area:** Economic Growth · Development Economics · Macroeconomics

A comparative examination of long-run economic growth in Bangladesh and Malaysia using the Solow Growth Model. The study considers differences in capital accumulation, productivity, and economic growth between the two economies.

**Research focus:**

* Solow Growth Model
* Capital accumulation
* Productivity
* Long-run economic growth
* Bangladesh–Malaysia comparison

---

### [Social Media Advertisement & Consumer Perception – Bangladesh](./Social-Media-Advertisement-and-Consumer-Perception/)

**Area:** Consumer Behaviour · Applied Research · Primary Data

A survey-based study examining university students' perceptions of social media advertising in Bangladesh.

The research involved questionnaire design, primary data collection, and analysis of consumer responses.

**Research methods:**

* Survey design
* Questionnaire development
* Primary data collection
* Statistical analysis
* Consumer behaviour analysis

---

### [Debt Crisis Analysis: Sri Lanka, Lebanon & Venezuela](./Debt-Crisis-Sri-Lanka-Lebanon-Venezuela/)

**Area:** Macroeconomics · International Economics · Development Economics

A comparative examination of major sovereign debt crises in Sri Lanka, Lebanon, and Venezuela, focusing on their underlying economic causes, policy responses, and consequences for economic stability and development.

**Research focus:**

* Sovereign debt
* Macroeconomic instability
* Fiscal policy
* Economic crises
* Comparative economic analysis

---

### [Effects of Ride-Sharing on Employment](./Effects-of-Ride-Sharing-on-Employment/)

**Area:** Labour Economics · Digital Economy · Employment

An examination of the relationship between the expansion of ride-sharing platforms and employment, considering how digital platforms can affect labour-market participation and employment opportunities.

**Research focus:**

* Labour markets
* Employment
* Digital platforms
* Gig economy
* Labour-market dynamics

---

### [Race for Rare Earths: USA & China](./Race-for-Rare-Earths-USA-China/)

**Area:** International Economics · Trade · Economic Policy

An examination of the economic and strategic competition surrounding rare-earth resources between the United States and China, with emphasis on international trade, resource dependence, and economic policy.

**Research focus:**

* International trade
* Strategic resources
* Resource dependence
* US–China economic relations
* Trade policy

---

### [Economic Consequences of Inadequate Access to Drinking Water](./Economic-Consequences-of-Inadequate-Access-to-Drinking-Water/)

**Area:** Development Economics · Environmental Economics · Public Policy

An examination of the economic consequences associated with inadequate access to safe drinking water, considering implications for health, productivity, development, and resource allocation.

**Research focus:**

* Development economics
* Environmental economics
* Public policy
* Resource allocation
* Economic development
